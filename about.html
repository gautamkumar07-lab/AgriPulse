About AgriPulse

Our story

AgriPulse began with a simple question: why do farmers still guess about water, weather and prices? We combine sensors, forecasts and market data into one clear dashboard.

Mission

Help every farmer make confident daily decisions with trustworthy, easy-to-read data.

Vision

A connected countryside where technology lifts yield, income and sustainability together.

Technology

Soil and weather sensors, satellite and drone imagery, and models for growth, disease and price forecasting.

Smart agriculture goals

30%less water used
+18%average yield gain
50kacres monitored

Growth timeline

2022 Prototype sensors tested on 20 farms.
2023 Weather and market modules launched.
2024 AI advisor and disease detection added.
2026 50,000 acres on the platform.
